What we collect

Training profile. Your equipment, schedule, experience level, goals, and logged lifts — used to generate your sessions and rankings.

Usage data. Basic product analytics (screens viewed, sessions completed) used to improve the app.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your training data or photos for advertising. We do not share your information with third parties except the service providers required to run the app (payment processing by Apple; subscription infrastructure by Superwall).

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple under the Apple Media Services Terms. We receive subscription status only — never your payment details.
